What this does and does not know

Survival numbers passed a calibration gate on last year's real 180 picks in this league: expected calibration error 0.025 against a 0.10 threshold. They run slightly conservative — players survived a touch more often than predicted — so if anything this nudges you to take now rather than wait.

No manager here has a measured personality. Tested on those 180 picks, 0 of 12 managers had a mean reach distinguishable from the league pool, and positional mix was near-uniform. So every manager's mean behaviour is the ADP prior, stated openly. Only dispersion survived: LewisS19 tracks ADP tightly, mstasik loosely. Those two are modelled; nobody else is.

Board value is unreliable past about pick 120 — measured, not guessed. Late rows are dimmed and carry a startable-rate instead, from real 2025 weekly scores.

It reads the draft; it never picks for you. No write access exists in this page.
